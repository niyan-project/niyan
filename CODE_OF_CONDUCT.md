# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Niyān contributors and maintainers commit to making participation welcoming and harassment-free regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or technical choices made in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to correction.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review threads, community spaces, and public interactions where someone represents Niyān. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, warnings, or other material that violates it and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer using the private contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not use a public issue when doing so would expose a target or reporter. Maintainers will limit disclosure to what is necessary to investigate and respond, avoid conflicts of interest, and protect the privacy and safety of everyone involved.
